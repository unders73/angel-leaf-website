# Book-page decorative artwork

Built with the built-in image-generation tool, using each original cover as its style/palette reference. Only the surrounding decorative frames are generated. Covers and sample pages are original book assets. Final transparent WebP paths and prompt specifics:

## activity

`assets/books/activity/frame.webp`

Pink, lilac and white with touches of yellow and black. Hand-drawn pink hearts, purple musical notes, yellow stars, pink heart-topped light sticks, small colouring pencils and doodle flourishes. Match the cover's playful girly activity-book scribbles; do not use turquoise as a main colour.

## fun-facts

`assets/books/fun-facts/frame.webp`

Turquoise/teal aqua, mint, pink accents, white and yellow stars. Headphones, vinyl record, turquoise microphone, pink heart lightstick, musical notes and playful quiz-like geometric doodles. Precisely the cover's energetic music-object illustration style.

## fan-journal

`assets/books/fan-journal/frame.webp`

Forest green, leafy dark green, mint green, lemon yellow, cream white and black outlines. Green hearts, musical notes, yellow stars, green brush strokes, small open journal and pen. Absolutely no pink or purple. Match the green cover's personal music-journal feel.

## release-tracker

`assets/books/release-tracker/frame.webp`

Vivid red, black, yellow, cream white. Red hearts, black notes, yellow stars, black vinyl record, red cassette, red-and-black headphones, microphone, pink-red heart lightstick and small rating stars. Match the red cover's music-release and review identity.

## studio-literacy

`assets/books/studio-literacy/frame.webp`

Royal blue, sky blue, lemon yellow, white and black; only tiny coral accents. Match the book's angular blue/yellow pencil-scribble frame exactly in spirit: open books, pencils, pen pots, speech bubbles, envelopes, paper sheets, yellow stars, a few small music notes. Emphasise reading/writing materials; NOT the generic pink/turquoise headphones/music activity border.

## world-literacy

`assets/books/world-literacy/frame.webp`

Royal/navy blue, bright yellow, sky blue, crisp white and black with tiny coral accents. Match this book's dense angular literacy scribble border: books, pencils, paper sheets, envelopes, speech bubbles, yellow stars, small music notes. Stronger dark blue/yellow contrast echoing its world-tour stage cover. Reading/writing objects, no pink dominant and no heart-lightstick activity-book theme.

## studio-numeracy

`assets/books/studio-numeracy/frame.webp`

Turquoise cyan, coral red, yellow, white and crisp black outlines. Match this numeracy cover's graphic border: calculator, ruler, protractor, set square, pencils, notebook, bar chart icons, tiny stars/hearts/music notes. Numeracy objects and bright maths-study atmosphere, clean hand-drawn brushstrokes.

## world-numeracy

`assets/books/world-numeracy/frame.webp`

Coral red, turquoise cyan, yellow, black and white. Match this book's concert/world-tour maths cover: stage lights and little speaker corners, ruler, calculator, charts and geometry triangles mixed with musical notes, small stars and hearts; energetic diagonal red/cyan/yellow scribbles. Distinct from the studio book through stage-light and concert details.

Shared prompt: Create one landscape 1536 × 1024 decorative frame, narrow continuous artwork around all four edges, transparent empty centre, crisp hand-drawn outlines. No characters, faces, covers, logo, text, headings, UI or watermark. Decoration remains at edges and corners so real HTML and authentic book pages remain separate. Frames use CSS nine-slice round repeat to preserve natural icon proportions on different screens.
